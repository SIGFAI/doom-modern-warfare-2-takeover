Bring Modern Warfare 2 into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I dropped Modern Warfare 2 into Doom: masked operators, a Juggernaut, killstreaks, and a tactical nuke that wiped the room.

## Install

Copy mod.pk3 next to GZDoom and run: gzdoom -iwad freedoom2.wad -file mod.pk3 (any Doom II IWAD works). Kill 2, 4 and 6 enemies in a row for a care package, a Predator missile strike and a tactical nuke.
